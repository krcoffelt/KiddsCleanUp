## Main Risks And Gaps

### Junk Removal Is Still Not Establishing Relevance

These tracked queries remain outside the top 100 on both desktop and mobile:

- junk removal kansas city
- junk removal near me
- furniture removal kansas city
- mattress removal kansas city
- trash removal kansas city
- haul away junk kansas city
- junk removal overland park
- junk removal olathe
- junk removal shawnee ks
- junk removal lenexa

The URLs are live, return 200 responses, appear in the sitemap, and have internal links. The next question is whether Google has indexed them and which URL Google associates with each query. Search Console should answer that before more copy is added.

### Broad Demolition Intent Is Volatile

- `demolition contractors kansas city`: 28 desktop / 35 mobile
- `demolition company kansas city`: 27 desktop / 27 mobile
- `residential demolition kansas city`: 79 desktop / 77 mobile
- `commercial demolition kansas city`: 17 desktop / 22 mobile
- `house demolition kansas city`: 34 desktop / 35 mobile

The site has overlapping demolition pages. The general demolition page should own the broad contractor/company terms, while `/services/residential` should own residential demolition and `/services/commercial` should own commercial demolition. Search Console landing-page data is needed to confirm whether pages are competing with each other.

### Water Pages Need Time And Clear Differentiation

Burst pipe cleanup entered the top 10 quickly, but water damage cleanup and basement water cleanup remain outside the top 100. Water mitigation and flood cleanup appeared during the week and then dropped out. These pages were newly deployed, so monitor them before considering consolidation.

### AI Citations Improved, But Brand Recognition Did Not

AI link presence rose to 22%, which is an encouraging early signal after the structured-data and page expansion. Mention presence remains 0%, so the remaining work is primarily corroboration outside the website: reviews, business listings, local links, consistent profiles, and explicit brand/service associations.

## Phase 2: Page Changes - After Search Console Review

### A. Strengthen The Main Junk Removal Page

Target URL: `/services/junk-removal`

Changes:

- Add one concise pricing/quote-factors section covering volume, weight, access, stairs, labor, and disposal.
- Add a service-boundaries section that clearly states what the crew takes, what requires advance discussion, and any items it does not accept.
- Add 2-3 real Kansas City project examples with city, job type, scope, and outcome.
- Add original junk-removal photos with specific alt text and visible captions.
- Add a customer review tied specifically to junk removal, if permission exists.
- Keep the current links to item pages and city pages.

Success condition: impressions begin growing for `junk removal kansas city` and related non-brand terms, with `/services/junk-removal` as the ranking URL.

### B. Differentiate Item-Removal Pages

First pages: `/services/bulk-trash-pickup`, `/services/yard-debris-removal`, `/services/appliance-removal`, and `/services/furniture-removal`.

The generated pages currently share much of the same structure and phrasing. Give the first four pages distinct information based on actual job requirements:

- bulk trash: curbside alternative, multi-item pickups, property-manager use cases, scheduling expectations
- yard debris: branches, storm debris, fencing, outdoor material, seasonal cleanup, excluded organic/regulated material if applicable
- appliance removal: refrigerators, freezers, washers/dryers, disconnect requirements, refrigerant handling boundaries
- furniture removal: inside pickup, stairs, disassembly, sofas, sectionals, office furniture, donation/disposal approach if true

Do not expand every out-of-index item page at once. Start with pages already receiving impressions or rankings, then use the result as the template for the remaining pages.

### C. Resolve Demolition Page Cannibalization

Assign one primary query family to each URL:

| URL | Primary query family |
| --- | --- |
| `/services/demolition` | demolition company/contractors Kansas City |
| `/services/residential` | residential demolition Kansas City |
| `/services/commercial` | commercial demolition Kansas City |
| `/services/house-demolition` | house demolition Kansas City |
| `/services/interior-demolition` | interior demolition Kansas City |

Changes:

- Align each title, H1, intro, internal anchor text, and schema service type with its assigned query family.
- Reduce mixed cleanout language on the residential page if Search Console shows the wrong URL ranking for residential demolition.
- Add a comparison section to the main demolition page that routes users to residential, commercial, interior, and structure-specific services.
- Add a real project example to the main, residential, and commercial pages.

### D. Promote Near-Winners Into Top 3

Priority URLs:

- `/services/deck-removal`
- `/services/lead-safe`

### E. Improve City Junk Pages With Verifiable Local Evidence

The four city pages already have city-specific headings and examples, but the examples are generic use cases rather than documented local work.

Changes:

- Add one real job or customer example per city when available.
- Add original photos from that city or clearly relevant nearby work.
- Link each page from its matching `/service-areas/{city}` page using a descriptive in-body link.
- Add city-specific reviews only when the reviewer/job location can be verified and permission exists.
- Avoid adding neighborhood lists solely for keyword coverage.

Priority order: Olathe, Overland Park, Lenexa, Shawnee.

### F. Continue The Water Cluster Carefully

Keep `/services/water-mitigation` as the parent and use the burst-pipe page as the benchmark.

Changes:

- Clarify the distinct intent of mitigation, extraction, flood cleanup, water damage cleanup, and basement water cleanup.
- Add equipment, response-process, service-area, and availability details only where operationally accurate.
- Add real water-job proof and before/after photos.
- Link water child pages contextually from basement cleanouts and relevant city pages.
- If a child page has no impressions after 4-6 weeks and materially duplicates another page, consolidate it with a redirect rather than maintaining thin overlap.
