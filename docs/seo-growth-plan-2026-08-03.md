## Purpose

This plan turns the August 3 ranking report into a measured implementation program. It covers technical SEO, page targeting, content proof, local authority, AI visibility, conversion tracking, verification, and reporting.

The latest deployment was live for only three days of the report period. The plan therefore protects existing gains, delays unnecessary rewrites, and uses Search Console evidence to decide which pages should be improved, consolidated, or left unchanged.

## Strategic Objectives

1. Protect and strengthen the existing top-three demolition and lead-safe rankings.
2. Establish `/services/junk-removal` for the core Kansas City junk-removal query family.
3. Move city junk-removal pages from positions 15-53 toward the top 10.
4. Stabilize cleanout and construction-debris rankings that currently swing by 40-60 positions.
5. Clarify and consolidate the water cluster using Search Console evidence.
6. Increase local authority through real project proof, reviews, citations, profiles, and links.
7. Move AI brand mentions above 0% and recover AI link presence above 20%.
8. Connect ranking improvements to qualified calls, texts, and quote submissions.

## Guardrails

- Do not rewrite top-three pages during the stabilization window unless a factual or technical problem is found.
- Do not create a separate `near me` page. The main junk-removal page and Google Business Profile should serve that intent.
- Do not create more city pages until the current city pages have verified impressions, rankings, and unique local proof.
- Do not publish invented projects, testimonials, neighborhoods, certifications, response times, or service claims.
- Do not add review or aggregate-rating schema unless the reviews are visible on the page and meet Google guidelines.
- Do not add `sameAs` URLs until the profiles are verified and controlled by the business.
- Do not consolidate or redirect a page based only on one weekly ranking report.
- Use 21-28 days of Search Console data for content decisions unless indexing or canonicalization is clearly broken.

## Workstream 2: Establish Core Junk Removal

The main `junk removal kansas city` and `junk removal near me` queries remain outside the top 100 even though city junk pages now rank. This indicates that Google recognizes the local city pages but has not yet assigned enough authority or relevance to the metro parent page.

### Diagnostic Gate

Inspect `/services/junk-removal` in Search Console and classify it:

#### Case A: Not Indexed Or Wrong Canonical

- confirm 200 response
- confirm indexable robots directive
- confirm self-referencing canonical
- confirm sitemap inclusion and last crawl
- inspect rendered HTML
- request indexing after fixing the specific issue

#### Case B: Indexed With No Non-Brand Impressions

- confirm internal links from homepage, footer, city pages, item pages, and cleanout pages
- add real project proof and original images
- strengthen Google Business Profile junk-removal services
- obtain service-specific reviews and local citations
- evaluate whether the page is too broad compared with ranking competitors

#### Case C: Impressions Exist But Position Is Poor

- compare title/H1/intro with actual query intent
- analyze competing result types: local pack, directories, national brands, or local companies
- add clearer service boundaries, quote factors, job types, and proof
- improve links from high-authority pages using natural junk-removal anchors
- address weak CTR only after the page reaches a meaningful impression volume

#### Case D: Another Site URL Ranks Instead

- identify the competing internal URL
- reduce overlapping targeting on the weaker page
- direct internal anchors toward `/services/junk-removal`
- consolidate only when both pages serve materially duplicate intent

### On-Page Deliverables

- 2-3 verified Kansas City junk-removal project examples
- original junk-removal photos with useful captions and alt text
- visible service-specific customer proof
- clear accepted-material and advance-discussion guidance
- links to the four city junk pages and strongest item/cleanout pages

## Decision Rules

### Improve A Page When

- it is indexed and receives impressions
- the intended page ranks but remains below the target range
- the page lacks verified proof, clear service boundaries, or useful intent coverage
- the ranking trend is stable enough to evaluate

### Leave A Page Alone When

- it holds a stable top-three or top-10 position
- the latest release is less than 21 days old
- fluctuations do not correspond to lost impressions or conversions
- no factual, technical, or intent problem is present

### Consolidate A Page When

- it has no meaningful impressions after 4-6 weeks
- another internal URL consistently owns the same query
- the service cannot be made operationally distinct
- content remains substantially duplicative

### Create A New Page When

- Search Console shows a distinct query group
- the business genuinely provides the service
- the intent cannot be satisfied by an existing page
- unique proof and useful content are available
- internal links and ongoing ownership are planned
